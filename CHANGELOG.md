# Changelog

Each release publishes its section here as the GitHub release notes.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-14

First release.

- Flip between two display arrangements, side by side and stacked, from the menu bar, with a right-click on the icon, or with a global shortcut (`⌃⌥⌘D` by default).
- The menu bar icon draws the arrangement you're in.
- Every change shows a brief confirmation on the screen your pointer is on, and the pointer stays where it was on that screen.
- Save your own arrangement into either slot.
